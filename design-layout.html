Design review

Portfolio plan and layout

What top software engineer portfolios contain, what yours is missing, and a proposed layout for the new Home page. Nothing is built yet. Review this, tell me what to change, and then we start.

1. Research

What the top portfolios do

I opened four well-known developer sites in a browser and read the 2026 hiring guides. The four sites are very different, which is useful: they show the range of what works.

Brittany Chiang

Two columns. A fixed left column holds the name, role, one-line pitch, section links and social icons. The right column scrolls through About, Experience, Projects and a link to the full résumé. Each job lists dates, a short description and its tech as tags.

Take: a clear pitch that stays on screen, dated experience with tech tags.

Lee Robinson

Almost all text. A short bio with his current and previous employers, then a dated list of essays and notes. No project grid at all; the writing is the proof.

Take: a short bio that names employers, and a dated writing list.

Josh Comeau

Leads with a featured article and a read-more link. The site is organised around teaching content rather than a list of jobs.

Take: a featured piece on the landing page.

Rauno Freiberg

Extremely minimal: name, one line on who he is, links to Projects, Craft and Field Notes, socials, and a copy-email button. Ends on a short set of principles.

Take: one-line identity, copy-email button, short principles.

Hiring guides read: CareerFoundry, Proxify, Maven Analytics, DataExpert. These are vendor and course blogs, so I used only the points that several of them agree on.

5. Your call

Decisions before I build

  1. Grey and white paletteApplied as you asked: white and light-grey pages, charcoal and grey for the hero and dark sections, floating nav kept. Tell me if you want the greys warmer, cooler or darker.
  2. Which section order?I proposed A to J. Tell me what to move, drop or add.
  3. Which project is the featured case study?Talk2DB is my suggestion. I need its real problem, approach and result from you.
  4. Include Writing in the first build?Skipping it means a faster first release. It can come in phase two.
  5. Your real contentI will not invent results, quotes or numbers. Send what you have, and anything missing stays hidden instead of showing a placeholder.
